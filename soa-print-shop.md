# SoA Print Shop

### About the SoA Print Shop <a href="#about-the-soa-print-shop" id="about-the-soa-print-shop"></a>



The SoA Print Shop is dedicated to supporting the University of Miami School of Architecture and its affiliated departments by providing high-quality, wide-format printing services. Our mission is to empower students, faculty, and staff with reliable, efficient, and cost-effective solutions for academic, research, and event needs.

*   **Wide-Format Printing:** We specialize in producing posters for projects, expos, and capstone presentations, offering a range of sizes and media types to suit diverse requirements. Recent invoices highlight our work for College of Engineering project posters and senior expo materials, demonstrating our commitment to academic excellence.

    \
    _&#x45;xamples:_ 36" x 48" project posters, 24" x 36" expo posters, biology research prints, and seating chart displays.
